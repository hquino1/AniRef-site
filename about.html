About AniRef

Practice turns effort into progress.

AniRef is a drawing practice app built around one idea: the artists who improve are the ones who keep showing up. We make showing up easier.

Why we built it

Drawing from reference is one of the most reliable ways to get better. In practice, though, the routine falls apart in small ways. References are scattered across a dozen apps and boards. Sketches end up in a camera roll next to everything else. And because nothing keeps track, it is hard to see that you are improving at all, which makes it easy to stop.

AniRef puts the whole loop in one place. Find a reference, draw it, capture each stage as you go, and let the app remember the rest: how long it took, what you drew from, and every day you practiced.

What we believe

  • Consistency beats intensity. A short session every day does more than a long one once in a while. Daily goals, streaks and reminders exist to support that, not to pressure you.
  • Progress should be visible. Seeing a month of attempts side by side is the best motivation there is.
  • Artists deserve credit. References belong to the people who made them. When you export a drawing, the artist is credited, and references from search providers are never redistributed in your exports.

Our promises to you

  • Your drawings are yours. You keep all rights to what you draw, including the right to sell it.
  • Your uploads stay private. Photos you add are shown only to you. We don't publish them, sell them, use them in marketing, or use them to train AI models.
  • We don't sell your data. We don't sell your personal information or share it for targeted advertising.
  • We never post for you. Connecting Pinterest or DeviantArt only lets you browse your own boards and collections. We never post or change anything on your account.
  • You can leave anytime. Download your whole library as a zip, and delete your account right from the app.

The details are in our Privacy Policy and Terms of Use.

Get in touch

AniRef is an independent app. Questions, ideas and bug reports are all welcome at app.aniref@gmail.com, or use Send Feedback in the More tab of the app.
