Getting started

What is AniRef?

AniRef is a drawing practice app. You find a reference, draw from it while capturing each stage, and the session is saved as an entry in your library. Over time your library, streak and activity charts show how much you've practiced and how far you've come.

Do I need an account?

Yes. Your library is saved to your account so it's safe if you change phones or reinstall. You can sign up with an email address and password, or sign in with Apple or Google.

How old do I need to be to use AniRef?

You must be at least 18 years old, or the age of majority where you live if that is higher.

Can I see the intro guide again?

Yes. Open the More tab and tap Replay Guide.

Does AniRef have dark mode?

Yes. Turn it on or off from Dark Mode in the More tab.

References

Where do references come from?

A single search runs across several sources, including Pexels and Unsplash. You can also connect your Pinterest or DeviantArt account to browse your own boards and collections, or use photos from your device.

What happens when I connect Pinterest or DeviantArt?

AniRef can show you your own boards and collections so you can use them as references. We never post or change anything on your account. You can revoke AniRef's access at any time from your Pinterest or DeviantArt account settings.

Can I use a reference image outside AniRef, or sell a drawing made from one?

Your drawings are yours. Reference images, however, belong to the people who made them, and using them in AniRef doesn't give you any rights to them. Pexels and Unsplash publish their own licenses, while images on Pinterest or DeviantArt usually belong to individual artists who haven't licensed them for reuse.

Drawing from a reference is a common way to learn, but a close copy of someone else's work can infringe their rights, especially if you publish or sell it. Check the source's license before using a reference or a close copy commercially. See Section 4 of the Terms.

What are reference groups?

A reference group is a saved stack of references you can reuse across sessions, ideal when you want to practice the same poses or angles every time. The free plan includes 1 group; Pro allows up to 30.

Drawing sessions & your library

How does a drawing session work?

Pick one or more references and start drawing. As you go, snap a photo at each stage, such as the sketch, line art and color. Each stage is timed automatically, so when you finish, the session is saved as an entry with a timeline of your drawing.

What's the difference between an entry and an attempt?

An entry is a drawing saved to your library, along with its reference. An attempt is another go at that same drawing. Retry any entry whenever you want another pass and compare the results. The free plan allows 3 attempts per entry; Pro allows up to 10.

How do streaks and daily goals work?

You choose the minimum number of minutes you want to draw each day. Meet that goal and your streak continues. You can also set a streak difficulty of Beginner, Intermediate or Veteran from Streak Difficulty in the More tab.

How do I turn daily reminders on or off?

Use the Notifications switch in the More tab to turn daily reminders on or off. You can also manage notification permissions in your device's settings.

Is there a home screen widget?

Yes. The streak widget keeps your drawing streak and today's goal on your home screen. See Streak Widget in the More tab for setup help.

How do I share or export my drawings?

Any drawing can be exported as a page with the reference beside it and the artist credited. You can also build a Progress Book from the More tab, which bundles a month of drawings with their dates, stages and time spent.

Share cards made from a photo you uploaded show only your drawing, not the photo.

Can I download a backup of my library?

Yes. Go to More → Download My Data. You can download your whole library, a single year, or a single month as a zip file, with each attempt and stage at full resolution. Photos you uploaded are included; references from search providers aren't copied, since their licenses allow viewing but not redistribution.

Plans & billing

What's the difference between Free and Pro?

Free includes up to 50 saved entries, 5 pinned entries, 3 attempts per drawing, 1 reference group, and activity for the current week and month.

Pro gives you unlimited entries and pins, up to 10 attempts per drawing, up to 30 reference groups, and your full activity history.

How much does Pro cost?

In the US, Pro is $4.99 per month or $39.99 per year. Prices vary by country, and the App Store always shows the price for yours before you subscribe.

Is there a free trial?

Yes, Pro comes with a 7-day free trial for eligible accounts. The trial turns into a paid subscription at the end unless you cancel at least 24 hours before it ends. Trial eligibility is decided by the App Store.

How do I cancel my subscription?

Subscriptions are managed by Apple, so you cancel through your App Store account: open the Settings app, tap your name, then Subscriptions, and choose AniRef. You can also tap Manage Subscription in the More tab.

Deleting the app or your AniRef account does not cancel your subscription. After you cancel, you keep Pro until the end of the period you've already paid for.

Can I get a refund?

Refunds for App Store purchases are handled by Apple under its own policies, and we're unable to issue them ourselves. You can request one at reportaproblem.apple.com.

I paid for Pro but the app still shows Free.

Open the More tab and tap Restore Purchases. Make sure your device is signed in to the same Apple account you subscribed with. If Pro still doesn't appear, contact us.

What happens to my drawings if Pro ends?

Nothing is deleted. You keep every entry, pin and reference group you made while on Pro. If you're over a free plan limit, you just won't be able to add more until you're back under it or upgrade again.

Account & privacy

Who can see my uploads and drawings?

Only you. Photos you add are stored in your account and shown only to you. We don't publish them, sell them, use them in marketing, or use them to train AI models. Nothing is shared unless you choose to share it yourself.

Do you sell my data or show ads?

No. We don't sell your personal information or share it for targeted advertising. We use a small set of service providers to run the app, which are listed in our Privacy Policy.

Does AniRef use analytics?

Yes. We use PostHog to understand how the app is used and to find and fix problems, including session recordings. Text you type, such as your email and password, is masked and not recorded, and so is your device's photo picker. Where the law requires it, we ask for your consent first. The details are in Section 1 of the Privacy Policy.

I forgot my password.

On the sign-in screen, tap Forgot password? and we'll email you a link to reset it. If you signed up with Apple or Google, sign in with that option instead.

How do I delete my account?

Open the More tab and tap Delete Account. This deletes your account and the content in it from our active systems. If you want a copy of your drawings, use Download My Data first.

Remember to cancel any subscription in your App Store settings, since deleting your account doesn't cancel it. Can't access the app? See Support.
