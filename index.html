How it works

Three steps, every session

Each drawing session follows the same simple loop, so showing up is the only hard part.

1

Find a reference

Search a few sources at once, or pull from your own photos. Pick several and they come into the session together.

2

Draw it, stage by stage

Snap the sketch, the line art, the color. Each stage is timed for you, so the session builds into a timeline.

3

Watch it add up

Every session becomes an entry in your library and keeps your streak alive.

Features

Everything your practice needs

From finding the right pose to seeing a whole year of drawing at a glance.

Find refs your way

One search runs across Pexels, Unsplash and more. Connect Pinterest or DeviantArt to browse your own boards, or use photos from your library.

Every drawing becomes an entry

Capture a photo at each stage as you draw. Every stage is timed, and you can retry a drawing later as a new attempt.

Everything you finish is kept

Entries stack up under the day you drew them, with the reference and the time attached. Search your library, or pin the ones you want back fast.

Reference groups

Stack several references into a saved group and reuse it across sessions, ideal when you want the same poses or angles every time.

Your work, ready to send

Export any drawing as a page with the reference beside it and the artist credited, or bundle a month into a progress book.

Your week, month and year

Every minute you log lands in your activity: this week in a chart, a recap of the month, and a heat map of every day you showed up.

Streaks and daily goals

Set how many minutes you want to draw each day and pick a streak difficulty: Beginner, Intermediate or Veteran.

Daily reminders

Choose a time and AniRef will nudge you to draw. Reminders are scheduled on your device and easy to turn off.

Streak widget

Put your drawing streak and today's goal on your home screen, always in view.
