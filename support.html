Email support

Tip: in the app, More → Send Feedback is the quickest way to report a bug or suggest an idea.

Manage or cancel Pro

Subscriptions are billed and managed by Apple.

  1. Open the Settings app and tap your name.
  2. Tap Subscriptions, then AniRef.
  3. Choose a plan or tap Cancel Subscription.

Restore purchases

Subscribed but seeing the free plan?

  1. Sign in to the same Apple account you subscribed with.
  2. In AniRef, open the More tab.
  3. Tap Restore Purchases.

Download your data

Get your drawings as a zip file, by library, year or month.

  1. Open the More tab.
  2. Tap Download My Data.
  3. Pick what to include and save the file.

Delete your account

This deletes your account and its content from our active systems.

  1. Open the More tab.
  2. Tap Delete Account and confirm.
  3. Cancel any subscription in your App Store settings. Deleting your account doesn't cancel it.

Can't access the app? Submit a data request or email app.aniref@gmail.com from the address on your account.

Privacy requests

To access, correct or delete your personal information, submit a data subject access request or email us. See your privacy rights.
